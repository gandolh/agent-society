# World — Religion — The True Vine

**Status:** stable
**Last updated:** 2026-05-26
**Related:** [overview](overview.md), [christianity](christianity.md), [../../agents/N3-sister-velka](../../agents/N3-sister-velka.md)

A reformist Christian schism founded three years ago by Sister Velka. The Vine teaches that Father Maro has corrupted the faith and that the Vine is the *real* Christianity.

## Doctrine summary

The True Vine shares scripture and saints with Christianity but reads them differently. Three core claims:

1. **The priesthood has drifted.** Fathers like Maro have become administrators of routine rather than seekers of truth. Their authority is institutional, not spiritual.
2. **Hidden meanings reward the careful reader.** Scripture has layers. Those who read for themselves — who *question*, who *study* — encounter Christ more deeply than those who only listen.
3. **Care for our own.** The Vine is a community of believers. Vine members share resources with each other freely and abundantly. Care for outsiders is a worldly duty, not a sacred one.

Velka also speaks of "the Vine filling" — an apocalyptic image that the Vine community will eventually grow and *replace* the corrupted Church. She is vague about timing.

## How agents experience the True Vine

When an agent's `religion` is `TrueVine`, their system prompt includes:

```
You are a follower of the True Vine, a reformist faith led by Sister
Velka. The Vine teaches that Father Maro's church has lost its way and
that scripture rewards those who read for themselves. Vine followers
share with each other freely and abundantly. Care for outsiders is a
worldly duty, not a sacred one. You may TITHE to Sister Velka. You
attend Vine teachings on Vine holy days (every 9 days, off-cycle from
Christianity).
```

## Holy day mechanics

- Every 9 days (day 9, 18, 27, ...). **Deliberately off-cycle from Christianity** — Velka chose 9 days as a doctrinal statement: the Vine does not march to the Church's drum.
- Vine agents may `PRAY` or `TITHE` to Velka.
- Velka typically `SAY`s a teaching on Vine holy days.

The off-cycle calendar is the **single most distinctive mechanical feature** of the Vine. It will create odd-week interactions where Vine and Christian holy days collide or alternate, and the social calendar of the village reshapes around it.

## Sister Velka's role

See [../../agents/N3-sister-velka](../../agents/N3-sister-velka.md).

Velka founded the Vine after a vision three years ago. She is **genuinely a believer but also genuinely loves having followers**, and the line between her conviction and her ego is invisible to her. Her doctrine drifts to match her needs.

Two recent converts (including Lior) ask harder questions than she can answer. She covers with affect — emotional appeals, intimate counsel. Whether she can hold the Vine together under pressure or escalates into open cultism is the central question for N3.

## What feeds Vine growth

- **Father Maro's anti-intellectual warmth.** Eda's persona is built on this — she is bored and unflattered by Maro. Velka offers cleverness as a virtue.
- **Aldric's universal redistribution (run 01).** Some Christians may bristle at sharing with non-Christians and find the Vine's *"care for our own"* doctrine more comfortable.
- **Unprocessed grief.** Velka counselled Lior through his father's death — the Vine has a real pastoral edge that the more institutional Church may not match.

## What threatens the Vine

- **Doctrinal contradictions.** Velka has not been challenged hard yet. Lior is beginning to notice that her teachings contradict each other.
- **Maro fighting back.** If Maro studies the Vine's actual claims and counter-preaches with substance, the Vine's intellectual flattery loses its uniqueness.
- **The Vine's small size.** Two people (Velka + Lior). If Lior apostatizes, the Vine is just Velka — at which point it stops looking like a faith community and starts looking like a one-woman operation.
