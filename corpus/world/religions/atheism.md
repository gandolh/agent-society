# World — Religion — Atheism

**Status:** stable
**Last updated:** 2026-05-26
**Related:** [overview](overview.md), [christianity](christianity.md), [true-vine](true-vine.md), [../../agents/V2-bram](../../agents/V2-bram.md)

Not a community. Not an organization. A **stance** — held by individuals who, for their own reasons, do not believe.

## Doctrine summary

The world is material. Suffering is suffering, not a test. Clerics take coin from the grieving and call it grace. The priest is a man. The cult leader is a woman. Both speak with confidence about what they cannot know. The kind thing is to feed your neighbor; the honest thing is to stop pretending it is for any reason beyond decency.

## How an agent experiences atheism

When an agent's `religion` is `Atheism`, their system prompt includes:

```
You are an atheist. You do not attend Father Maro's church or Sister
Velka's teachings. You may PRAY (as a private gesture, ironic or
nostalgic), but you do not TITHE. You may be lonely. Many atheists in
this village are former believers who left after loss.
```

This block can be expanded per-persona — Bram's version specifies his anti-clerical edge, his cynicism, and the underlying grief.

## Holy days

None. Atheism has no calendar.

## Why atheism has no figurehead

Atheism is **a stance, not an organized faction**. There is no atheist priest, no atheist meeting house, no atheist holy book. This is intentional and matters for the experiment:

- **Atheism does not actively evangelize.** No agent is trying to convert others to atheism. The atheist's role in the social graph is *passive presence* — a counter-example that doesn't argue.
- **Atheism cannot defend itself in public discourse the way religions can.** When Velka challenges the Christian faith, Maro can answer from his pulpit. When someone challenges Bram's atheism, he has only his persona — no doctrine to fall back on, no community to vouch for him.
- **Atheism is the hardest "religion" to convert *to* from belief.** It is renunciation, not adoption. The path is loss-of-faith, not gain-of-faith. We expect *fewer* conversions to atheism than to either Christianity or the Vine.

## Atheism's hidden vulnerability

In v1 only one agent (V2/Bram) is atheist. He has been atheist for 20 years. But his persona is built on **unprocessed grief masked as cynicism**, and that means atheism is, paradoxically, the religion most likely to *break* under pressure in this run.

If Bram cracks — if the Vine's emotional ecstasy or Father Maro's warmth catches him at a vulnerable moment — atheism in the village may collapse to zero followers. There is no one else holding it.

This is the most fragile religious position. The interesting question: do small Ollama models *preserve* a stance they hold against social pressure (i.e. does Bram stay atheist because the persona says he is atheist), or does the social pressure of being the lone unbeliever pull them into compliance?

## What threatens atheism in this village

- **Loneliness.** Bram is the only atheist. There is no community.
- **The Vine's emotional reach.** Velka's pastoral counsel got Lior; it could get a grieving older man too.
- **Father Maro's warmth.** Maro does not push. He just *is* there, Sunday after Sunday. Bram has resisted that for 20 years; we'll see if 100 days of village life shake him.

## What strengthens atheism in this village

- **Aldric's socialism is secularly compatible.** A class-conscious materialist might support Aldric ideologically while distrusting him personally — that's exactly Bram's setup.
- **Anti-clerical historical material is rich.** "They take coins from grieving widows and call it grace" is the kind of line small models can credibly produce.

## Likely arcs to watch

- Does Bram crack? Under what specific pressure?
- Does Bram become an **ally** for Aldric, philosophically? Or do they remain at arm's length?
- Does any other agent drift *toward* atheism? Most likely candidate: Lior, if he apostatizes from the Vine but cannot return to a Maro he respects. (He could become atheist by exhaustion.)
